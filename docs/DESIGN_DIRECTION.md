# A daily companion for life on Mars

The owner's direction, September 2026: imagine living on Mars ten years from now. Inexpensive Android phones are everywhere, and this wallet is a familiar part of daily life. It should carry the optimism and affection of the people who dreamed of Mars before that future arrived.

Build on Yanina's original work and its Expanse influence. Refine the existing identity through typography, spacing, materials and behavior. Keep the Martian landscapes, black surfaces, geometric lettering, civic identity and warm orange.

## Design decisions

- Use orange to identify the next important action and the selected state. Give secondary controls quiet charcoal surfaces and clear white text.
- Use Orbitron for identity and occasional display moments; Chakra Petch for readable instructions, names, balances and controls. Show financial precision without crowding the screen.
- Give receive requests, payment confirmation and the civic ID enough space to feel deliberate. Use plain labels such as “Receive Marscoin”; keep internal route names out of the interface.
- Make every state understandable: saved locally, connecting, ready to review, submitted, awaiting confirmation, or ready to retry. A connection failure must not erase a wallet or restart a publication from scratch.
- Invite permissions at the relevant action. Explicit Paste and payment-alert controls replace unexpected prompts while opening the app or showing an address.
- Design for inexpensive Android hardware: large touch targets, restrained effects, compressed media, and useful behavior on weak or intermittent connections. Test small screens and physical Android devices before release.

## Current first pass

Citizen directory tabs now have quieter inactive states and larger targets. Duplicate identities are collapsed. The civic connection/terms card has a defined text hierarchy and an explicit action. Receive, fee and publication labels describe user actions, with fees expressed consistently in MARS and zubrins.

This is the beginning of the visual refinement. The wallet overview, civic ID, application screens and shared navigation still need a coordinated typography, spacing and accessibility pass, reviewed on Android as well as iOS.
